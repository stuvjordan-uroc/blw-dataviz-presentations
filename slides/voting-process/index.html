Persons who want to vote apply to be registered. Election administrators check eligibility of persons who apply to be registered and register those eligible. Identifying information (such as address, signature, photo, etc. stored) for each registered voter.

Persons who want to cast votes show up at the polling place. Election admins check identity of each person who shows up against registration records. Persons confirmed to be registered are allowed to vote. Election admins track which registered voters have voted, to prevent any one from voting more than once.
